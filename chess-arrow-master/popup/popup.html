How to Use

  • Open Chess.com in your mobile browser
  • Tap the 🎯 floating button to open the toolbar
  • Enable the extension and choose a mode
  • Single tap – highlight a square
  • Long press + drag – draw an arrow
  • Two-finger drag – force annotation
  • Double tap – remove highlight
  • Triple tap – remove nearest arrow

Supported Browsers

  • Kiwi Browser
  • Lemur Browser
  • Quetta Browser
